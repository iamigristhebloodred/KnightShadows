About KnightShadows

Building a better digital world since 2024

Our Story

Founded in 2024 by a group of open source enthusiasts, KnightShadows began as a small collective of developers concerned about the increasing centralization and surveillance on the internet. What started as a weekend project to create a privacy-focused browser has grown into a global movement with thousands of contributors.

We've evolved from a single project to a thriving ecosystem of tools that put users first. Our journey has been guided by one simple principle: technology should serve people, not corporations.
