Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

🌐

Open & Transparent

All our code is open source and publicly available. We believe in radical transparency and community-driven development. Every decision, every line of code, every financial transaction is open for scrutiny.

  • All code on GitHub under OSI-approved licenses
  • Public board meetings and decision logs
  • Transparent financial reporting
  • Community voting on major decisions
🔒

Privacy First

We build tools that respect user privacy by default. No tracking, no data selling, no compromises. Privacy isn't a feature—it's the foundation.

  • Zero data collection by default
  • End-to-end encryption where possible
  • No third-party trackers
  • Transparent data policies
🤲

Accessible to All

Technology should be free and accessible to everyone, regardless of background or financial status. We prioritize accessibility in all our projects.

  • Free forever with no premium tiers
  • WCAG 2.1 AA compliance standard
  • Multi-language support
  • Low-bandwidth optimizations
🌱

Community Driven

Our projects are built by volunteers around the world who share our vision for a better internet. The community leads, we facilitate.

  • Community-elected maintainers
  • Mentorship programs for new contributors
  • Regular community calls and events
  • Recognition programs for contributors
⚖️

Ethical by Design

We consider the ethical implications of everything we build. Technology should empower, not exploit.

  • Ethical review for all new features
  • Bias testing in AI/ML components
  • Sustainable infrastructure choices
  • Digital rights advocacy
🚀

Innovation Through Collaboration

We believe the best solutions come from diverse perspectives working together. Collaboration beats competition.

  • Cross-project knowledge sharing
  • Partnerships with other FOSS projects
  • Research collaborations with academia
  • Open innovation challenges
